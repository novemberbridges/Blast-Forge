This page can't open YouTube links or take sound from them. Load an audio file of the song that you have, and set Start to a spot where the beat you want begins. Detection listens for kick, snare and cymbals and fills the pattern at the Bars, Beats and Grid set below. Treat the result as a first draft: in a dense metal mix it will miss and add hits. Tick “Play the song with the pattern” to hear both together, then fix steps on the grid or record over it on the pads. If the tempo is wrong, set it in the top bar and press Use my tempo.

Stems carry each channel's tune, decay, filters, volume and pan, with no reverb or master processing, so they line up sample-accurately at bar 1 and you can mix them in the DAW. The full mix includes everything. MIDI uses General MIDI drum notes on channel 10: kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, open hat 46, ride 51, crash 49, china 52, toms 48 / 45 / 41.
